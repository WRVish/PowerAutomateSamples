# ⚡ Power Automate Samples

This repository is a growing collection of **Microsoft Power Automate** flow samples covering a wide range of real-world business automation scenarios. The goal is to provide practical, ready-to-use flows that help individuals and organisations save time, reduce manual effort, and get the most out of the Microsoft Power Platform.

Whether you are just getting started with Power Automate or looking for advanced patterns, you will find samples here that you can import, adapt, and build upon for your own needs.
